CLASSROOM ACTIVITY — AEROSPACE TRACK

Signal Watch

Six unidentified contacts are approaching Earth orbit. Your team runs each one through the pattern recognition pipeline — turning raw sensor noise into a correct call — before Mission Control has to act.

Facilitator notes
Time: ~20–30 minutes for 6 rounds. Run in teams of 2–4 on one shared screen, or have every team play on its own device.

Concept: every round pushes a team through the same five stages — Acquire → Preprocess → Extract Features → Classify → Decide/Act — using a real aerospace scenario (debris, satellites, meteors, near-Earth objects, spacecraft faults, and a collision-avoidance call).

Discussion prompts after playing: Which stage caused the most wrong answers, and why? What happens if you skip preprocessing and classify raw, noisy data? Why does the same "debris" classification sometimes lead to "just log it" and other times to "maneuver now"?

Leaderboard: scores saved from this activity are visible to every team using this link/file — good for a live class leaderboard, so mention that before teams save their scores.
